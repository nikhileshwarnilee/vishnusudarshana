# Project Proposal & Cost Estimation

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

- This project is a complete business website with an owner control panel for daily operations.
- It allows customers to book services, reserve office visit tokens, register for events, make payments, track their requests, and receive updates in an organized manner.
- It gives the business owner and staff one central place to manage appointments, service requests, events, payments, customer records, staff schedules, and website content.
- Main problem solved: it reduces manual phone calls, diary work, queue confusion, payment follow-up, and event coordination effort.
- Main users of the system are:
- Customers / devotees
- Reception / front desk team
- Service and operations staff
- Event team
- Business owner / admin

## 2. KEY FEATURES (USER SIDE)

- Online service booking: customers can choose a service category and submit their details easily from mobile or desktop.
- Service selection with pricing: customers can clearly see available service options and the amount before confirming.
- Online payment support: paid bookings can be completed immediately, reducing collection delays.
- Booking reference and status tracking: customers can check the progress of their request using their mobile number or booking reference.
- Secure file or report access: service-related reports can be shared safely after customer verification.
- Offline token booking: customers can reserve an office visit token in advance and avoid standing in long queues.
- Live token status: customers can see the current token movement before visiting, helping them plan better.
- Event listing and registration: customers can view upcoming events, package options, seat availability, and register directly.
- Waitlist support: when an event is full, interested customers can still join a waiting list instead of being lost.
- Flexible event payments: bookings can be completed through full payment, advance payment, or later balance payment.
- Event booking management: customers can track booking status, pending balance, ticket status, and cancellation or refund progress.
- Digital entry ticket: confirmed event customers can download a professional ticket for easy entry.
- Knowledge Centre: the website includes useful articles and updates that build trust and increase repeat visits.
- Daily spiritual content: visitors can check daily Panchang, horoscope, and day significance updates, encouraging regular engagement.
- Easy mobile access: the website works smoothly on mobile and can be saved for quicker repeat use.

## 3. ADMIN PANEL FEATURES

- Central dashboard: the owner gets a summary of bookings, appointments, payments, events, visitors, and pending work.
- Appointment control: appointments can be accepted, scheduled, completed, and managed date-wise.
- Date blocking: unavailable dates can be blocked to avoid overbooking or operational clashes.
- Service request handling: all online and offline requests can be reviewed, updated, and followed up from one place.
- Product and service management: the business can add, edit, arrange, activate, or deactivate offerings easily.
- Token management: the admin can control date-wise token limits, city-wise availability, and live token visibility.
- Reception log: walk-in and phone visitors can be recorded and tracked properly.
- Event management: events can be created with package options, seat limits, registration periods, and booking rules.
- Event registration control: bookings, pending payments, waiting list cases, cancellations, refunds, and check-in records can all be handled from the panel.
- Event entry management: the event team can verify customer entry quickly using booking details or ticket scan.
- Event reports: the system gives event-wise numbers for bookings, payments, waiting list, cancellations, refunds, and attendance.
- Billing and invoicing: invoices can be created, customer payments can be recorded, dues can be tracked, and reminders can be sent.
- Customer database: the business can maintain a searchable list of customers for follow-up and repeat business.
- Client enquiry and follow-up records: the system stores customer history, enquiry notes, and follow-up details for better long-term relationship management.
- Staff schedule management: work schedules can be assigned and shared with team members.
- Website content management: blogs, notices, banners, and important site information can be updated from the panel.
- User management: the owner can create staff accounts and control who can manage which area.

## 4. BUSINESS BENEFITS

- Saves staff time by reducing manual booking, payment follow-up, and status checking.
- Improves customer experience through faster booking, clear communication, and less waiting.
- Increases revenue opportunities through online booking, event registration, and smoother payment collection.
- Reduces no-show and confusion through date control, reminders, live token visibility, and trackable requests.
- Gives the owner better control over operations, customer records, and pending work.
- Builds trust through professional presentation, digital tickets, useful content, and regular updates.
- Supports future growth by allowing multiple staff users and multiple business functions in one system.

## 5. MODULE BREAKDOWN

- Public Website: business introduction, service discovery, contact details, and information pages.
- Online Service Booking: service selection, customer form filling, pricing, and booking submission.
- Payment and Tracking: payment collection, booking reference generation, and customer status tracking.
- Offline Token System: date-wise office token booking, live queue visibility, and better reception handling.
- Event Management: event listing, registration, package selection, seat control, waiting list, and ticket flow.
- Billing and Dues: invoice creation, due tracking, payment recording, and reminder support.
- Customer Database and Records: customer history, enquiry tracking, and follow-up management.
- Staff Operations: dashboard view, work schedules, staff permissions, and team coordination.
- Content and Engagement: blogs, daily spiritual updates, and notice management.

## 6. DEVELOPMENT EFFORT ESTIMATION

- Estimated effort if built from scratch: **85 to 110 working days**
- Planning and business discussion: **5 to 7 days**
- Design and page approval: **8 to 12 days**
- Main build work: **55 to 70 days**
- Checking and corrections: **12 to 15 days**
- Launch, training, and handover: **5 to 6 days**

## 7. TEAM REQUIRED

- Developer: **1 senior developer minimum**; **2 developers recommended** for faster delivery on a project of this size.
- Designer: **1 designer** for page layout, customer experience, and visual consistency.
- Tester: **1 tester** for complete flow checking before launch.
- Project Manager: **1 part-time project manager or business coordinator** for approvals, planning, and delivery tracking.

## 9. TIMELINE

- Estimated total timeline: **18 to 20 weeks**
- Week 1 to 2: planning, business flow mapping, sample page approval
- Week 3 to 6: public website, online service booking, payment flow
- Week 7 to 9: tracking flow, secure delivery, token booking, live queue
- Week 10 to 14: event registration, tickets, waiting list, entry flow, and cancellation handling
- Week 15 to 17: billing, customer database, staff management, admin controls, and reports
- Week 18 to 20: final checking, corrections, launch, training, and launch support

## 10. MAINTENANCE & SUPPORT (OPTIONAL ADD-ON)

- Includes:
- Issue fixing and small corrections
- Minor content, notice, and banner updates
- Booking and payment support checks
- Monthly review of reports and admin flow
- Basic staff support and troubleshooting
- Routine health checks after launch
